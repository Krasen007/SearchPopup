What's Changed
- Fuel-efficiency conversions now work in both directions, including `8 l/100km` to mpg and slash-units like `km/h` written before the number
- Switching the crypto target currency now clears old prices immediately, so conversions never mix rates from the previous currency
- Currency conversions refresh when cached rates expire, not only after an error, while the popup still appears instantly and updates live
- Hovering the popup pauses its 3-second auto-hide until you move away
- Conversion failures now show a single calm "using cached data" note instead of technical error text
- Removed internal performance recorders and simplified popup styling with no visible change
